Frequently Asked Questions

Find solutions fast. Get the most out of your mobile money experience.

How can I send money via USSD? ▼
Dial *150*01# → Select 1 to send money → Enter receiver's number → Enter amount → Enter PIN to confirm.
How can I send money via the Mixx by Yas App? ▼
Open App → Select Send Money → Choose contact → Enter amount → Confirm with PIN.
What if I send money to the wrong person? ▼
Call Customer Care at 100 OR use Self-Care → Transaction Reversal within 12 hours.
What is Nivushe Plus? ▼
A cash loan product allowing Mixx by Yas customers to receive short-term loans from 1,000 TSh to 2,000,000 TSh.
How do I get a loan? ▼
Dial *150*01# → Select 7 (Financial Services) → Loans → Nivushe Plus → Follow prompts.
